Whole Chicken Dinnerfor a Full Table

Crispy herb-butter chicken, jasmine rice, and garlicky spinach—the complete family dinner from Jadon’s cook-along.

Serves 930 min prep45–60 min cook + 20 min rest

Measured recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 whole chickens (4–5 lb each)
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp olive oil for the chicken
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt for the chicken
  • 2 tsp black pepper for the chicken
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 4 cups dry jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 4½ cups water, or the amount directed for your rice cooker
  • 1 tbsp olive oil for the spinach
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 16 oz fresh spinach
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • ¼ tsp kosher salt for the spinach
  • ¼ tsp black pepper for the spinach

Amounts not stated in the video are standardized for the listed yield.

Cook in this order

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a large roasting pan or two sheet pans with foil. Put each chicken breast-side down, cut along both sides of the backbone, remove it, flip the chicken, and press firmly on the breastbone until it lies flat.
  2. Pat both chickens completely dry. Mix the softened butter with 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1 tablespoon salt, 2 teaspoons pepper, garlic powder, paprika, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Gently loosen the skin over the breasts and thighs. Rub half of the seasoned butter under the skin and the rest over the outside of both chickens.
  4. Set the chickens skin-side up without crowding. Roast for 45–60 minutes, until the thickest part of the breast and thigh reaches 165°F. Spoon over the pan juices and rest 20 minutes before carving.
  5. While the chicken roasts, rinse the jasmine rice until the water is nearly clear. Cook it with 4½ cups water, or the amount required by your rice cooker.
  6. Warm 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium heat. Cook the garlic until fragrant and lightly golden. Add the spinach and turn it until wilted. Finish with the vinegar, ¼ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon pepper.
  7. Carve the rested chicken. Serve it with jasmine rice and spinach, spooning the buttery pan juices over the chicken or rice.
Food-safety checkChicken is ready when the thickest breast and thigh both register 165°F on an instant-read thermometer without touching bone. Do not rely on color or timing alone.
