Whole Chicken Dinnerfor a Full Table

Crispy herb-butter chicken, jasmine rice, and garlicky spinach—the complete family dinner from Jadon’s cook-along.

Serves 8–1025 min prep45–60 min cook

Measured recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 whole chickens (4–5 lb each)
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, plus 1 tbsp for the spinach
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt, divided
  • 2 tsp black pepper, divided
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 4 cups dry jasmine rice, rinsed
  • Water as directed for your rice cooker
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 16 oz fresh spinach
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar

Cook in this order

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Put each chicken breast-side down. Cut along both sides of the backbone with kitchen shears, remove it, flip the chicken, and press firmly on the breastbone until the chicken lies flat.
  2. Pat both chickens completely dry. Mix the softened butter with 2 tablespoons olive oil, 2 teaspoons salt, 1 teaspoon pepper, paprika, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning.
  3. Gently loosen the skin over the breasts and thighs. Rub half of the seasoned butter under the skin and the rest over the outside of both chickens.
  4. Set the chickens flat in a foil-lined roasting pan. Roast for 45–60 minutes, until the thickest part of the breast and thigh reaches 165°F. Rest 20 minutes before carving.
  5. While the chicken roasts, rinse the jasmine rice until the water is nearly clear. Cook it in a rice cooker with the amount of water required by your cooker.
  6. Warm 1 tablespoon olive oil over medium heat. Cook the garlic until fragrant and lightly golden. Add the spinach and turn it until wilted. Finish with apple cider vinegar, the remaining salt, and the remaining pepper.
  7. Carve the rested chicken. Serve it over jasmine rice with spinach and spoon the buttery pan juices over the chicken and rice.
Food-safety checkChicken is ready when the thickest breast and thigh both register 165°F on an instant-read thermometer. Do not rely on color or timing alone.
