Honey Garlic Lime Shrimpwith Rice & Broccoli

Sweet, bright shrimp with jasmine rice and broccoli. The sauce comes together while the rice cooks, and the shrimp needs only a few minutes in the pan.

Serves 415 min prep15 min cook

Measured recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1½ cups dry jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 12 oz frozen broccoli florets
  • 4–5 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup honey
  • 2 limes, zested and juiced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
  • ½ tsp black pepper, divided
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ½ cup water for steaming the broccoli

Cook in this order

Method

  1. Cook the rinsed jasmine rice in a rice cooker or according to the package directions.
  2. Whisk the minced garlic, honey, lime zest, lime juice, ½ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon pepper in a bowl. Set the sauce aside.
  3. Put the broccoli and ½ cup water in a covered skillet over medium heat. Season with garlic powder, ¼ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon pepper. Steam for about 3 minutes, then uncover and cook until tender.
  4. Heat the avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shrimp in one layer and toss for 3–4 minutes, until they begin to curl and turn opaque.
  5. Pour in the honey garlic lime sauce. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring, then add the butter 1 tablespoon at a time until the sauce is glossy and coats the shrimp.
  6. Divide the rice and broccoli among four bowls. Spoon the shrimp and sauce over the rice and serve right away.
Food-safety checkShrimp is done when it is opaque and reaches 145°F. Remove it from the heat promptly so it stays tender.
