Chicken and PotatoesEasy Family Dinner

Bone-in chicken thighs and Yukon gold potatoes with paprika, garlic, salt, and pepper—a big-family dinner built from everyday ingredients.

Serves 915 min prep40–50 min cook

Measured recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 lb bone-in skin-on chicken thighs
  • 4 lb Yukon gold potatoes, cut into 1½-inch pieces
  • 3 tbsp avocado oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tsp paprika

Amounts not stated in the video are standardized for the listed yield.

Cook in this order

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line two large rimmed sheet pans with foil.
  2. Pat the chicken thighs dry. Toss them with 1 tablespoon avocado oil and half the salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  3. Toss the potatoes with the remaining 2 tablespoons avocado oil and the remaining seasonings.
  4. Spread the potatoes across both pans and arrange the chicken thighs skin-side up among them, leaving room so everything roasts instead of steaming.
  5. Roast for 25 minutes, turn the potatoes, and rotate the pans. Continue roasting for 15–25 minutes, until the potatoes are browned and tender and the chicken reaches 165°F.
  6. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes before serving with the potatoes.
Food-safety checkChicken thighs must reach 165°F in the thickest part without the thermometer touching bone.
