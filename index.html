Who’s cooking

I’m Jadon.
I learned by feeding people.

I cook dinner for my family of nine. That taught me what actually matters: food that tastes good, fills people up, uses normal ingredients, and doesn’t leave a mountain of dishes.

Everything here comes from a real kitchen and gets eaten by real people. I’m not trying to make cooking look complicated. I want to help you build enough skill to walk into the kitchen and know what to do.
